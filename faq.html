The idea

What Next10 is, and who it's for

What is Next10, and how is it different?

Next10 helps you stay on track with your goals — not just check off another habit without knowing where it's leading. Most habit trackers show you a streak. Next10 shows you progress on the goal itself, over time, so you always know how far you've actually come.

Who is Next10 for?

Anyone serious about their goals — people who want to actually achieve them, not just track them.

Why 10 years?

Ten years is enough time to change your lifestyle — how you live, and who you become. Nobody builds something incredible overnight, and no habit changes your character overnight either. Ten years is the timeframe where real, lasting change actually happens.

How it works

The mechanics behind the goals

How do I connect my long-term goals to daily habits?

That's up to you. There's no one-size-fits-all formula — you decide which daily habits move you toward the goals that matter to you.

How does Next10 avoid the "streak trap"?

Next10 is built around consistency over the long run, not unbroken streaks. You won't lose the physique you built overnight, and you won't build it overnight either. Dipping from a streak now and then is normal — it's not a failure state.

What happens if I miss a day or break a streak?

Nothing happens. Next10 doesn't punish missed days. The app is built around your long-term goals, not the short-term completion of one or two habits — so skipping a day on one habit doesn't set back the bigger picture.

Can I update my 10-year goals later?

Yes — you can update your goals whenever you want.

Does Next10 use AI?

No. We keep AI out of Next10 on purpose — for privacy, and because AI can't replace your own intention. Next10 is built to be intentional: we don't want you entering some data and letting an algorithm hand you a plan you never actually follow through on. Real progress comes from you engaging with the plan, not from outsourcing it.

Platform & access

Where you can use it

What platforms is Next10 available on?

Next10 is currently iOS-only. An Android version is in the works.

Does Next10 work offline?

The core features work offline, so you can keep logging habits and goals without a connection. Anything that needs the cloud will simply sync the next time you're back online.

Is there a web or desktop version?

Not currently — Next10 is iOS-only for now.

Pricing

Free to start, premium to go deeper

Is Next10 free?

Yes, Next10 is free to start. Advanced stats and other premium features are available with a subscription.

What's included in premium?

Premium unlocks advanced stats, additional app icons, and more premium tools as we roll them out.

Is there a trial period?

How do I cancel my subscription?

Data & privacy

What happens to your data

Is my personal data secure?

Yes. Your data is stored in Firestore, Google's secure cloud database, and it's only ever used to give you the best possible experience in the app.

Can I delete my account?

Yes — you can delete your account any time from the app's settings.

Does Next10 share my data with third parties?

No. The only third parties involved are Firestore, for storing your data, and the App Store, for processing payments. Your data is never sold or shared for advertising.
