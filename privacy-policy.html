1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide to Us

We collect information you voluntarily provide to us when you join our waitlist, including:

  • Name - your first and last name
  • Email address - to communicate waitlist updates and marketing communications
  • Device platform - iPhone, Android, or both (to tailor launch communications)
  • Referral source - how you found out about Next10 (optional)
  • Feature suggestions - any optional feedback you provide

How Your Information Reaches Us

When you submit the waitlist form on next10.live, your data is transmitted directly to a Google Sheet maintained by us, using Google Apps Script. Google LLC acts as our data processor for this storage. Google's servers are located in the United States; we rely on Google's certification under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses to ensure your data receives equivalent protection to EU law.

Information Automatically Collected

When you visit our Site, we may automatically collect technical information, including browser type and version, operating system, referring URLs, device type and screen resolution, pages visited and time spent, approximate geographic location (country/region level), and anonymised IP address.

We do not collect your precise location, financial data, government identification numbers, or any special category personal data as defined under GDPR Article 9.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide for the following purposes:

  • Waitlist management — to record your place on the waitlist and verify the integrity of submissions.
  • Marketing and launch communications — to send you emails about the Next10 app, including: development updates, behind-the-scenes news, beta access invitations, launch announcements, post-launch product updates, new feature releases, and other information we believe will be useful to you as someone interested in the app. These emails are sent on the basis of your explicit consent given at signup. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of any email, or by emailing privacy@next10.live.
  • Service and security — to operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Site.
  • Analytics — to understand (in aggregated, anonymised form) how visitors use the Site, subject to your cookie consent.
  • Legal compliance — to comply with applicable laws and to detect, prevent, or address fraud or misuse.

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we rely on the following lawful bases to process your personal data:

  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)) - You joined our waitlist and gave explicit consent to receive marketing communications about Next10.
  • Legitimate Interests (Article 6(1)(f)) - Operating, maintaining, and improving our website and services, where such interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal Obligation (Article 6(1)(c)) - Compliance with applicable laws and regulations.

You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us at privacy@next10.live or using the unsubscribe link in any email we send you.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access - Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification - Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure - Request deletion of your personal data where we have no compelling reason to continue processing it.
  • Right to Restrict Processing - Request that we temporarily stop processing your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability - Receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object - Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent - Where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@next10.live. We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. In Germany, this is the relevant state data protection authority (Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz). In the UK, this is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

8. California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the CCPA as amended by the CPRA grants you additional rights:

  • Right to Know - What personal information we collect, use, disclose, or sell.
  • Right to Delete - Request deletion of personal information we have collected.
  • Right to Correct - Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to Opt-Out - We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination - We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights.

To submit a CCPA request, contact us at privacy@next10.live with the subject line "CCPA Request".

9. Automated Decision-Making

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you. Your waitlist position is determined on a first-come, first-served basis based on submission timestamp, without any algorithmic scoring.
